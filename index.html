Privacy Policy - Dear Emperor

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy describes how Dear Emperor ("the app," "we," "us") handles information. It's written to be accurate for the app as it works today, and will need a short update if what's described below changes later - see "Changes to this policy."

The short version

Most of what Dear Emperor tracks about your progress stays only on your device. A small part is synced to a server we control, so your streak and rank aren't lost if you reinstall the app or switch phones, and so a request you make from the app (like asking for a change of assignment) can reach us - this is tied to an anonymous identifier and the war name you choose in the app, never your real name or email, unless you choose to create an account (with Apple, Google or email). We also collect a small amount of anonymous usage data (which screens you open, whether you filed a report) to understand how the app is used. We never collect the free text you write yourself, like your personal goal.

What the app stores, and where

All of the following is stored only on your device, using the device's standard local storage (never transmitted anywhere):

  • The habit, wake time, and report time you chose during enrollment, and any personal goal or note you write
  • Your chosen background/appearance and language setting
  • Your Continental Blockade configuration (the apps you chose and your blockade windows), if you use that feature
  • Your campaign diary notes

This data is deleted if you uninstall the app, or immediately if you use the in-app "Definitive discharge" option in Settings, which erases it and returns the app to its starting state.

What's synced to our server

Your campaign progress - the war name you chose, current day, streak, rank, medals, regiment/marshal assignment, and report counters - is synced to a database we run on Supabase, so it survives a reinstall and so you can ask us (from the Libretto screen) to change your assignment. This is tied to an anonymous identifier generated on your device, not to your real name or email.

Global leaderboards

The app has public leaderboards (streak of reports, reports this week, battles fought, and the eight marshal corps). To make them fair, each nightly report is also recorded on our server with only its date, its outcome (won or lost) and your marshal - never the note you write. The server also keeps your time zone, so that a "day" ends at the same local time for everyone.

On the leaderboards, other players can see only your position, your war name, your rank and the number being ranked. They never see your goal, your notes, your email or any other data. Every player gets a random public identifier, used only so that a name can be reported or blocked; it is not linked to your account in any way visible to others.

You can leave the leaderboards at any time with "Hide me from rankings" on the leaderboard screen: you keep playing, but your name no longer appears. War names that are reported as offensive may be replaced with "Soldato" and a number. All of this is deleted together with the rest of your data when you use "Definitive discharge".

Optional account

Creating an account is optional and never required to use the app or to buy anything. If you choose to create one, you can sign in with Apple, with Google, or with an email address and password; the email address (and the name, if Apple or Google provides it) is stored by Supabase Auth, only so you can restore your progress on a new device and so we can recognize and help a subscriber who contacts us. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are never visible to us. See Supabase's privacy policy for how they handle this data on our behalf.

Anonymous usage analytics

We use PostHog to understand which parts of the app people actually use: which screens you open, and a fixed set of events (e.g. starting enrollment, filing a nightly report, earning a medal, ranking up, viewing the paywall, completing a purchase). These are always sent as categories or counts - for example, which habit category you picked, never the sentence you typed for your personal goal - and tied to the same anonymous identifier as above, never your name.

Continental Blockade (app blocking)

On iPhone, if you choose to use the Continental Blockade feature, the app asks iOS for Screen Time authorization (Apple's Family Controls framework) to let you block distracting apps during time windows you set. This works entirely through Apple's own on-device APIs: we never see which apps you have, which ones you've chosen to block, or how you use your phone. The list of apps you select is handled by Apple's system picker and never leaves your device.

On Android, the same feature uses Android's Accessibility service, which you turn on yourself after an in-app explanation. It is used only to know which app is being opened, so the app can cover the apps you chose during your blockade windows or timer. It does not read text or screen content, and nothing it sees leaves your device or is sent to us.

Notifications

The app schedules local notifications (e.g., your daily order, a reminder before your report window closes) directly on your device using the system's notification service. These are not push notifications from a server - nothing is sent to us to trigger them.

In-app purchases

If you subscribe to the Vecchia Guardia (Old Guard) tier or buy a book background, the purchase itself is handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play - we never see or store your payment details. To manage and verify subscriptions, we use RevenueCat, a subscription-management service; RevenueCat receives a device/subscriber identifier and your purchase and subscription status only. This is standard practice for apps that sell subscriptions and is limited to what's needed to know whether your subscription is active. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for how they handle this data.

What we don't do

  • We don't have advertising or third-party trackers of any kind - nothing here is used to advertise to you or to anyone else, on or off the app.
  • We don't ask for your real name, email, contacts, location, camera, or photos, and don't collect them, unless you choose to create an account (see "Optional account").
  • We don't sell your data, and don't share it with anyone beyond the named service providers above (Supabase, PostHog, RevenueCat), each of whom only processes it to provide their specific service to us.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects changes in a future update, this policy will be updated first, and the update will be reflected in the app's "What's New" notes.
